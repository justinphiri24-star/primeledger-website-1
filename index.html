Invoice discounting

Turn unpaid invoices into working capital now.

Waiting to be paid? Get cash against your invoices.

If you supply goods or services and your clients pay on 30, 60 or 90-day terms, invoice discounting releases that money early, so you can keep buying stock, paying staff and taking on new work.

  • Share the invoices you have issued to your clients
  • We review them and advance funds against their value
  • When your client pays, the advance is settled
